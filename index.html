❓ FAQ
What is $NCORE?▼
$NCORE is the in-game token of Nature Core. You earn it by farming with your tree. In the future it will be exchangeable for the real $NCORE jetton on TON blockchain.
How do I farm tokens?▼
Connect your wallet, select an NFT artifact, choose a garden and press Farm. Your tree will generate $NCORE for up to 6 hours per session. Come back to collect and start a new session!
How do I rent a garden?▼
Tap "Use Artifact" → choose a garden → press Rent. You'll confirm a TON payment in your wallet. Rental lasts 30 days and can be extended anytime. Mystic (1.5 TON), Infernal (2.5 TON), Ethereal (5 TON).
What is an artifact?▼
An artifact is your Nature Core NFT from the TON blockchain. Its rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary) determines a multiplier that boosts your farming speed. The rarer the artifact, the more $NCORE you earn!
Why did my tree wither?▼
Your garden rental expired! A withered tree cannot farm or be upgraded. Don't worry — your tree level and progress are saved. Simply renew the rental to restore your garden and continue farming.
